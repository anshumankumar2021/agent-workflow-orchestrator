# Volume definitions

- **Gross volume**: sum of amounts of transactions with status 'approved' or 'refunded' (refunded transactions were approved before being refunded). Declined transactions are excluded.
- **Net volume**: gross volume minus refunded amounts.
- **Approval rate**: approved transactions divided by (approved + declined) transactions. Refunded transactions count as approved for approval-rate purposes because they were approved first.
- Reporting periods are calendar months unless stated otherwise.
