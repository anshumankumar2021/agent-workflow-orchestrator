# Cross-border transactions

A transaction is **cross-border** when the card's issuing country (card_country) differs from the merchant's country.
**Cross-border share** = cross-border transactions ÷ all of the merchant's transactions (any status) × 100.
A cross-border share above 25% for a low-risk merchant triggers a manual review of the merchant's traffic sources.
Cross-border transactions carry an additional 1.0% assessment fee.
