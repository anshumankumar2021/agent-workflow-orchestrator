# High-risk merchant categories

These merchant category codes (MCCs) are high risk and are always assigned risk tier **high**:

- 4722 Travel agencies (long delay between purchase and service)
- 5967 Direct marketing - inbound telemarketing
- 7995 Gambling

High-risk merchants need enhanced due diligence at onboarding and a quarterly review. A rolling reserve of **10%** of settled volume is held for 180 days.
