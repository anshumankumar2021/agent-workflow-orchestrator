# Risk ticket priorities

- **P1**: chargeback ratio above 1.8%, or more than 5 open chargebacks on one merchant. Response within 2 business days.
- **P2**: chargeback ratio between 0.9% and 1.8%, or a sudden decline-rate increase. Response within 5 business days.
- **P3**: informational follow-ups. Response within 15 business days.

Only analysts may open tickets. Viewers can read data but cannot create or change tickets.
